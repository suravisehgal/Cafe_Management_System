The Velvet & Bean Story

Founded in 2024, Velvet & Bean was born from a deep reverence for small-batch coffee roasting, traditional slow food, and genuine hospitality. We source 100% single-estate Arabica coffee beans directly from fair-trade farmers in the Western Ghats and roast them on-site every morning.

Our kitchen prepares fresh hand-rolled pastas, stone-pressed panini sandwiches, and buttery French pastries from scratch daily without artificial additives or preservatives.
